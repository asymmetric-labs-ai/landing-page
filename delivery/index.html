Policy

Delivery.

AsymAI Labs Private Limited sells digital subscriptions only. Nothing is shipped. Effective 17 November 2026.

1. How access is delivered

Everything we sell is delivered online. When your payment is confirmed, the subscription is activated on the account you paid from and you can use it straight away in the app or on the web.

2. No physical delivery

We do not sell or ship physical goods, so there are no shipping charges, delivery addresses or delivery times.

3. If access does not start

If you have been charged and your subscription is not active, sign out and back in first. If it is still not active, write to support@asymmetriclabs.ai from the email address on your account with the order or payment ID. We will activate the subscription or refund the charge, as our Cancellation and Refund Policy sets out.

4. Where we sell

Subscriptions are sold to users in India and charged in Indian Rupees.
