About us

About Asymmetric Labs.

Asymmetric Labs is the trading name of AsymAI Labs Private Limited, an education technology company in India. We build learning software for people preparing for demanding examinations.

Our business

Three lines of work.

1. Asym

Our own product: a learning platform for UPSC Civil Services aspirants. It is free to use at the moment.

2. Hela distribution

Asymmetric Labs is the sole distributor of Hela in India. We sell Hela subscriptions to users in India and collect payment in Indian Rupees.

3. Services

We provide software development, content production and user support services for Hela.

Customers

Who we sell to.

Our customers are individual learners in India: UPSC aspirants for Asym, and medical students and clinicians for Hela. What we sell is a digital subscription, bought online with access given immediately. We do not sell physical goods.
