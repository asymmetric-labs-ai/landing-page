Our product

Asym.

Asym is a learning platform for UPSC Civil Services aspirants. It keeps study notes, previous year questions and practice tests in one place, on Android, iOS and the web.

What Asym includes

Read, practise, and see where you stand.

Study notes

Notes organised by subject and topic across the Prelims syllabus, written and reviewed by subject experts.

Previous year questions

Questions from past UPSC Prelims papers, each with an explained answer and a link back to the notes it draws on.

Tests

Practice by topic or build a custom test from the subjects and years you choose, then review every answer.

Current affairs

Current affairs material tied to the syllabus topics it belongs to.

Your progress

Bookmarks for notes and questions, and statistics on what you have attempted and how you scored.

Discussion

Comment on notes and questions, and report anything that looks wrong so it gets corrected.
