Policy

Cancellation and refunds.

This policy covers every subscription sold by AsymAI Labs Private Limited. Today that means Hela subscriptions bought in India; Asym is free to use at the moment. Effective 17 November 2026.

1. What you buy

We sell digital subscriptions. A subscription gives access to the product's paid content and features for the period you buy. Prices are shown in Indian Rupees, inclusive of GST, before you pay. Payments are processed by Razorpay.

2. Renewal

Hela. A Hela subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period, at the price shown when you subscribed, until you cancel.

Asym. Asym is free at the moment, so there is nothing to renew or cancel. If we introduce paid plans, a plan will run for the duration bought and will not renew automatically unless that is stated at purchase.

3. Cancelling

You can cancel a renewing subscription at any time before the next renewal date, from your account on hela.org or by writing to support@asymmetriclabs.ai. Cancellation stops future charges. Your access continues to the end of the period you have already paid for; the current period is not refunded on cancellation.

4. Refunds

In line with the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020:

  • Charged but not activated, or charged twice. If you are charged and the subscription is not activated, or you are charged more than once for the same order, we refund the failed or extra charge in full.
  • Service fault. If a technical fault on our side stops you from using the core paid features of your subscription for a continuous 72 hours or more after you report it, you may ask for a pro-rata refund for the affected period.
  • Change of mind. Because a subscription gives immediate access to digital content, we do not offer change-of-mind refunds once paid content has been accessed, except where the law requires it.

5. How to ask for a refund

Write to support@asymmetriclabs.ai from the email address on your account, with the order or payment ID and the reason. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, typically within 5 to 7 business days after Razorpay confirms the refund. Your bank may take longer to show it.

6. Purchases through an app store

If you bought a subscription through the Apple App Store or Google Play billing, cancellation and refunds are handled by that store under its own policies.
