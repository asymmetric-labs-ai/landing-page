Hela in India.

Hela is a medical knowledge platform for medical students and clinicians, owned and operated by Cistema Pte. Ltd., Singapore. AsymAI Labs Private Limited is its distributor in India.

The product

Clinical knowledge, kept current, with the evidence attached.

Hela is a maintained reference for medicine, written as dockets: structured notes on conditions, drugs and clinical thresholds, each statement carrying its sources. It is available on the web and as mobile apps, for users aged 18 and over.

Everything about the product, including its plans and current prices, is on hela.org.

Buying Hela in India

How payment works.

You choose a plan on hela.org, sign in, and pay through Razorpay by UPI, card or another method Razorpay offers. Access starts as soon as the payment is confirmed. The subscription renews at the end of each billing period until you cancel; after you cancel, access continues to the end of the period you have paid for.

The charge is made by AsymAI Labs Private Limited. Use of Hela itself is governed by Hela's Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. Billing, cancellation and refunds are covered by our Cancellation and Refund Policy.
